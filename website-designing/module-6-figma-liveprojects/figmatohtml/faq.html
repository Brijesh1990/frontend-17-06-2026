FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know about order delivery, size charts, return policies, and promo vouchers.

All orders placed before 3:00 PM are processed and dispatched within 24 hours. Standard domestic shipping arrives in 2–4 business days. Priority express shipping is also available at checkout for next-day arrival in major metro areas.
Once your clothes ship from our warehouse, you receive an automated SMS and email containing your live tracking link. You can also view live delivery updates inside the Rondovision mobile app.
We provide a 14-day hassle-free return and exchange guarantee. As long as items are unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags attached, our courier partner will pick them up from your doorstep at zero return fee.
Our hoodies and sweetshirts are tailored with a trendy, comfortable relaxed-fit silhouette. If you prefer an oversized aesthetic, we recommend ordering true to size. If you want a tailored slim-fit look, consider ordering one size down. Detailed chest and sleeve measurements are available on each product page.
During our Payday campaign (1 June – 10 June), spend a minimum of $100 on any eligible brands (H&M, OBEY, Lacoste, Levi's, etc.) and you will instantly receive a 30% off voucher code applied automatically at checkout or sent to your inbox.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, UPI / Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for domestic locations.
